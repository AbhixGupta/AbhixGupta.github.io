# Portfolio Customization Checklist

Before deploying your portfolio, update the following placeholders in `index.html`:

## Contact Information (Required)

Search and replace these placeholders in `index.html`:

### 1. Email Address
**Location:** Line ~801
**Find:** `your.email@example.com`
**Replace with:** Your actual email address

### 2. LinkedIn Profile
**Location:** Line ~805
**Find:** `https://linkedin.com/in/yourprofile`
**Replace with:** Your actual LinkedIn profile URL

### 3. GitHub Profile
**Location:** Line ~809
**Find:** `https://github.com/yourusername`
**Replace with:** Your actual GitHub profile URL

## Optional Customizations

### 4. Logo Text
**Location:** Line ~573
**Current:** `{ DevOps.dev }`
**You can change this to:** Your name, initials, or preferred branding

### 5. About Me Section
**Location:** Lines ~598-609
You can further personalize the bio if you want to add:
- Specific projects you've worked on
- Years of experience
- Special achievements
- Personal interests

### 6. Footer Copyright
**Location:** Line ~821
**Current:** `&copy; 2026 All Rights Reserved`
**You can change to:** `&copy; 2026 Your Name. All Rights Reserved`

## Quick Find & Replace Guide

Use your text editor's find-and-replace feature (Cmd+F on Mac, Ctrl+F on Windows):

1. **Email:** Find `your.email@example.com` → Replace with your email
2. **LinkedIn:** Find `yourprofile` in LinkedIn URL → Replace with your username
3. **GitHub:** Find `yourusername` in GitHub URL → Replace with your username

## Verification Checklist

Before deploying, verify:
- [ ] Email link opens your email client correctly
- [ ] LinkedIn link opens your profile
- [ ] GitHub link opens your profile
- [ ] All certifications are listed correctly (already done!)
- [ ] Tech stack reflects your skills (already done!)
- [ ] Services section matches what you offer (already done!)

## After Customization

Once you've updated your contact information:

1. Open `index.html` in your browser to test all links
2. Click each contact link to ensure they work
3. Follow the deployment guide in `DEPLOYMENT.md`
4. Push to GitHub and enable GitHub Pages

Your portfolio is ready to go live!
